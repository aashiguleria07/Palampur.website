DISCOVER PALAMPUR

A little piece of Himachal

Palampur is a beautiful hill town in the Kangra Valley, surrounded by tea gardens, pine forests and the spectacular Dhauladhar range.

From quiet walks through green tea estates to breathtaking mountain views, Palampur offers a refreshing escape from busy city life.

Explore traditional villages, peaceful monasteries, artistic communities and nearby adventure destinations. Every corner has something different to discover.
